**CICY SUMMIT 2026** — the Regional Congress of Graduate Students — takes place at the Centro de Investigación Científica de Yucatán (CICY) in Mérida, Yucatán, on November 19, 2026.

**Fátima Miranda Pestaña, Jaziel A. Carballo Tadeo, and Alejandro Molina Villegas** present the oral talk **"Reliable Data to Teach AI to Listen to Maya"**, about the reliable data needed to teach artificial intelligence to recognize and process Yucatec Maya speech.
