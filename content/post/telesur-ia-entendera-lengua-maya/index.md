**AI Will Understand — Maya Language Thanks to Researcher**

Yucatecan researcher brings Yucatec Maya to Lisbon: proposes a method for artificial intelligence to understand the language.

More than 774,000 Yucatec Maya speakers on the Peninsula face a daily paradox: while any phone easily recognizes English, Spanish, or French, the region's most widely spoken Indigenous language is practically invisible to technology. No virtual assistant understands it. No app recognizes it. Research presented in Lisbon, Portugal, proposes a method to start closing that gap.
